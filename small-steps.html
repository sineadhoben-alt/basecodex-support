Small Steps: English & Maths

Short, focused learning weeks, with a separate nickname, avatar and saved plan for each child.

Small Steps helps families explore primary English and Maths, including earlier foundations. A starting-point check suggests a first step. Five teaching sessions and a move-on check help guide the next week.

Made for everyday family learning

Children can choose their names and continue offline. Parents can review progress in a PIN-protected area. iPhone and iPad use the same Apple Account’s private iCloud database. A parent can optionally connect the same Google account on Android devices to sync the family through private Google Drive app-data storage. No Small Steps or ChatGPT account is required. Android sync does not connect to iCloud or to other Google accounts in a family group.

Currently being prepared for testing

The beta has 79 weekly catch-up routes across core primary English and Maths areas. The owner reviewed and approved the content on 29 October 2026. It is not a complete UK curriculum or phonics programme. Some early English and writing activities need adult involvement. Suggestions use authored rules and sampled responses; they are not a diagnosis or a guarantee of a suitable learning level. No payments or subscriptions are enabled.

External beta distribution is being prepared. When invited, please use fictional child profiles and keep children’s personal information and family backup files out of feedback.
