Small Steps: English & Maths

Short, focused learning weeks, with a separate nickname, avatar and saved plan for each child.

Small Steps helps families explore primary English and Maths, including earlier foundations. A starting-point check suggests a first step. Five teaching sessions and a move-on check help guide the next week.

Made for everyday family learning

Children can choose their names and continue offline. Parents can review progress in a PIN-protected area. A parent can optionally connect the same Google account on Android devices to sync the family through private Google Drive app-data storage. No Small Steps or ChatGPT account is required. Android sync does not connect to iCloud or to other Google accounts in a family group.

Currently being prepared for testing

The Android beta has 26 example weekly routes and partial curriculum coverage. It is not a complete UK curriculum or phonics programme. Some early English and writing activities need adult involvement. Suggestions use authored rules and sampled responses; they are not a diagnosis or a guarantee of a suitable learning level. No payments or subscriptions are enabled.

The closed test is not open yet. When invited, please use fictional child profiles and keep children’s personal information and family backup files out of feedback.
