Privacy Policy

Applies to Sky Piggies, Onward GCSE, Transfer Trainer NI (Transfer Trainer), GCSE English Trainer, PomPom Pets, Planet Patchers, WordQuest, Wormy Bop, Stacky Snails, Chip!, Stella’s World of Wow, Grammar Glow, Bert and the Great Oak, Transfer Ready NI, Sight Reader, Little Learning Trails and StudyBranches. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Short version: our apps don't collect your personal data. Everything an app stores — your progress, scores, settings and learning history — stays on your device and, where the app supports it, in your own private iCloud account. We can't see it, we don't sell it, and we don't share it.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not gather names, email addresses, contacts, location, advertising identifiers or usage-tracking profiles.

Data stored on your device

To work, an app saves your game or learning data (progress, scores, preferences) locally on your device. If the app offers iCloud sync, that data is stored in your own iCloud account under your Apple ID and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Stella’s World of Wow on Android saves discoveries, favourites, quiz progress and settings on the device. It does not require an account, internet access or cloud sync. Android device backups may be governed by your device settings; Stella’s World of Wow does not provide an app backup service.

Little Learning Trails — Maths & English

Little Learning Trails is intended for Northern Ireland P5–P7 children with parent or carer support. Each installation stores one learner’s nickname, answers, writing, project drafts, progress and settings locally. No child account is required. The app does not send this learning work to us or provide cloud sync between devices.

A local parent PIN restricts adult controls; it does not encrypt learning work. Device or operating-system backups may include app data according to your device settings. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage or choosing to reset progress can remove saved work. Avoid entering private information in learning activities. External links open only when a parent chooses them.

TestFlight and Google Play testing services handle tester information, diagnostics and feedback under their own privacy policies. If you send feedback, do not include a child’s name, school or private learning work. Participation in any supervised evaluation is arranged separately with the owner, with appropriate consent.

StudyBranches

StudyBranches is a device-local study notebook for secondary-school students, roughly 11–18. It organises learner-supplied material and self-review; it does not automatically mark answers or provide a complete syllabus.

Notebook content and device access

The app stores maps, notes, original imported text, source references, connections, flashcards, recall drafts, self-review choices and dates, recordings, edit history, recovery copies, timer settings and display preferences in the current installation’s local notebook. Learners may enter personal information in their own notes; StudyBranches does not transmit this notebook content to us, analytics services or AI services. We cannot access or recover it remotely.

No StudyBranches account is required. The app has no advertising or tracking SDKs and does not access contacts, location or a camera. Microphone permission is requested only when you choose Record a note. Recording is optional; refusing permission leaves text tools available. Voice notes are stored locally and included in notebook backups. Read-aloud uses the device’s speech features.

StudyBranches does not add its own notebook password or separate encryption layer. Access to your device or browser profile and to exported files controls who can read the notebook. Keep exported schoolwork and recordings private.

Imports exports and backups

Imported files are processed locally. The app reads files you select and exports content to destinations you choose through the platform’s file or sharing controls. If you choose an email service, cloud drive or another provider for an export, that provider handles the exported file under its own policies.

iPhone, iPad, Android and browser installations have independent notebooks. StudyBranches does not provide automatic cross-device sync or a developer-operated cloud backup. On iPhone and iPad, operating-system backups are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s services. The current Android app declares exclusions for app data in Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Use the app’s exported backups to transfer or preserve notebook content.

Retention and deletion

Notebook content remains in the local installation until it is removed there. Editing history and recovery copies can retain earlier work; deleting an idea is not a guarantee that all earlier copies have been removed. Uninstalling the native app, clearing its storage or clearing browser website data can remove the local notebook. Browser storage may also be evicted by the platform. Export a backup before removing an installation. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them until you remove them from those destinations. We do not hold a server copy of your notebook.

Support and platform services

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include for the enquiry and any follow-up. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail and remains separate from app notebooks; you can request its deletion by emailing bumblebopgames@gmail.com. Parents or guardians should contact us for children. Please do not include a child’s name, school or private learning work.

Current development previews do not start a paid trial or make purchases. Any future store purchase is handled by the relevant store, not by collecting payment details in StudyBranches. Apple and Google distribution or testing services may process tester details, diagnostics, transactions or feedback under their own policies. This policy will be updated if the app’s data handling changes. The website and support-email hosting information below also applies to StudyBranches.

Analytics & tracking

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Apple or Google may provide us with aggregated store or crash statistics under their own privacy policies. The Android app does not send us usage or personal data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the store you use: Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Stella’s World of Wow has no in-app purchases.

Children

Our apps are suitable for a general audience and do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Support enquiries should be sent by a parent or guardian. Please do not include a child’s full name, school or other personal information.

Your rights

Since app progress lives on your device (and, for apps with iCloud sync, in your iCloud), you are in control. Deleting Stella’s World of Wow on Android removes its locally stored progress. For an app with iCloud sync, you can also clear its iCloud data in device Settings. If you have questions about your privacy, email us any time.

When you contact support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so that we can respond to your enquiry. Please send only what is needed to explain the issue. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence.

This website

This support site uses no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information, including IP addresses, when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email is handled by Gmail; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
