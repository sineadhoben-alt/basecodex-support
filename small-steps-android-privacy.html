Small Steps: English & Maths — Android test version

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Developer: BaseCodex
Contact: transfertrainerni@gmail.com

Small Steps helps families practise English and Maths. This is an unfinished beta with partial curriculum coverage. Some early English and writing activities require adult involvement. Recommendations are based on authored rules and sampled responses; they are not a diagnosis or a guarantee of a suitable learning level.

What is saved

The app stores child nicknames, school systems and years, chosen avatars, assessment responses, lesson attempts, writing, weekly plans and device preferences in its private local storage. No child email address, date of birth or photo is required. Use fictional profiles during community testing.

Your data

There is no Small Steps or ChatGPT account. The app has no advertising, analytics SDK, connected AI service, payments or live email delivery. Learning records are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app uses Internet access for optional private Google sync. It does not request microphone, location or broad storage permissions.

Optional automatic google sync

A parent can connect Google sync in the PIN-protected parent area on each Android device. Google asks the parent to choose an account and grant access to Small Steps' hidden, private application-data folder. This permission does not give Small Steps access to unrelated Drive files. Profiles, learning progress, avatars, unfinished work and weekly plans are uploaded to that Google account and downloaded on connected Android devices using the same account. This does not connect to iCloud or to other Google accounts in a family group.

The Google account's identifier and email address are saved in private device settings to keep accounts separate. Short-lived Google access tokens are held in memory and by Google Play services; the app does not put them in learning records, exported backups or developer logs. No developer-operated server receives these tokens or learning records. A parent can disconnect sync and revoke access. Cloud records remain until removed through the Google account's Drive app-management controls. Offline learning remains available without Google connection. Google processes account and Drive data under its privacy policy.

Small Steps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only for the parent-requested family sync and account separation, not for advertising, selling data or training AI models.

Backup files

A parent can export a family file through the system file picker. The selected document provider handles that file under its own settings and privacy policy. Keep it private; do not include it in community feedback. Previously linked family files are kept as backups, not automatically imported into a different Google account.

Read-aloud and printing

Read-aloud passes the explanation text to your chosen Android text-to-speech engine. That engine may use network services according to your device settings; choose an installed offline voice if required. Printing passes the selected report to your system print service. These services have their own privacy practices.

Parent controls

A six-digit PIN protects the parent area on this device. It is stored as a salted digest, is not encryption and does not sync. Children can switch names and choose avatars without a PIN. Anyone using the device can choose any child profile. The PIN is not a separate secure account for each child. There is currently no forgotten-PIN recovery; keep it safe.

Control and deletion

Parents can remove a child profile in the parent area. Exported backups and copies held by a document provider must be deleted separately. Disconnecting a linked file does not delete it. Removing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress, so export any wanted work first. Cloud records can restore progress when that Google account reconnects. A different Google account opens a separate saved family.

Beta feedback

Google Play and your testing provider process tester enrolment, technical reports and feedback under their own policies. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you choose to send, and use it to respond and investigate. Do not send children's personal information, family backups or unredacted progress screenshots. Request deletion of support correspondence using the contact above; correspondence is retained only while needed to resolve the enquiry and beta issue.

Support website and changes

This public page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser and GitHub may process technical visit information, such as an IP address, under their own privacy practices. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Support email is handled through Gmail. We will update this page before releasing a change to these data practices.

This version is intended for supervised evaluation. It is not an exam-preparation guarantee, a complete phonics programme or a complete UK curriculum.
