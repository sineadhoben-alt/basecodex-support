Stella’s World of Wow for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026 for Android version 1.0 (1).

The app

Stella’s World of Wow is an offline discovery app for children aged 6 to 11. It has no accounts, adverts, analytics, tracking, chat or in-app purchases. It does not request Android internet, location, contacts, camera or microphone permission.

Data stored on your device

The app stores discoveries, favourite facts, quiz results, review timing, daily selections and sound preferences locally on the device. It does not send this information to us or a server. Android app backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

External references

Facts and games work without internet access. A grown-up may open a fact reference in an external browser after unlocking the device to enter the grown-ups’ area. Those websites and the browser have their own privacy practices.

Purchases and support

Google Play handles the purchase of the app under Google’s own terms and privacy practices. We do not see or store payment details. If a grown-up emails support, we receive the email address and message they choose to send so we can reply. Please do not include a child’s name, school or other personal information. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence.

Deleting progress

A grown-up can use “Reset discoveries on this device” in the device-unlocked area. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it also removes progress. There is no app account or server-side profile to delete.

Contact and changes

We will update this page and its date if the app’s data practices change. For privacy questions, email bumblebopgames@gmail.com.
