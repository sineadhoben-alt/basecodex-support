Common questions

How do I report a problem?

Email us with the app name and a short description of the steps that led to the problem. If a screenshot helps, remove any personal information before sending it.

Do I need an account?

The apps listed here work without creating an account with us. Apple services, such as purchases or optional iCloud features, use your own Apple account.

Where is my progress saved?

Game progress, settings and learning history stay on your device and, where an app supports it, in your own iCloud account. We cannot recover local progress for you. Please contact us before deleting an app to troubleshoot a problem.
