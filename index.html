StudyBranches

Created by a qualified, experienced teacher, StudyBranches helps secondary-school students organise their own notes with mind maps, flashcards and an optional focus timer. Current local test builds are development previews.

How do I add branches and details?
  1. Open a map and choose Add main branch for a big idea connected to your central topic.
  2. Tap an idea, then choose Add detail for a smaller idea connected to it.
  3. Use Back to map and My maps to return to your work.

The same ideas are available as a text list in Outline.

How do I use flashcards and the focus timer?

Choose Flashcards in your notebook to create a card set, or open an idea’s More idea tools and choose Make a flashcard. Try recalling an answer before revealing your notes, then compare and record your own confidence. Nothing is automatically marked.

Choose Focus timer to start, pause or resume a session and plan a break. The timer pauses when you leave the app; it does not provide background alarms.

How do I back up or move my notebook?

Use Help & backup → Export notebook backup. On the other installation, choose Choose notebook backup, inspect the preview, then choose Restore notebook content. Existing work is kept and conflicting references become separate copies. Backups with flashcards require StudyBranches 0.2 or later.

Each iPhone, iPad, Android and browser installation has its own notebook. There is no automatic sync. Keep regular exported backups; uninstalling the app or clearing browser data can remove local work.

For help, email bumblebopgames@gmail.com with “StudyBranches”, your device and app version, and a short description of the problem. Please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for a child and avoid sending private schoolwork or identifying details. See StudyBranches privacy information.

Common questions

How do I report a problem?

Email us with the app name and a short description of the steps that led to the problem. If a screenshot helps, remove any personal information before sending it.

Do I need an account?

The apps listed here work without creating an account with us. Apple services, such as purchases or optional iCloud features, use your own Apple account.

Where is my progress saved?

Game progress, settings and learning history stay on your device and, where an app supports it, in your own iCloud account. We cannot recover local progress for you. Please contact us before deleting an app to troubleshoot a problem.
