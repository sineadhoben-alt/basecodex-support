Sky Piggies for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Sky Piggies is an offline children's game published by BaseCodex. The Android app does not require an account or ask for a child's age. A player may optionally name and design a piggy.

Data stored on your device

The app saves game progress, scores, coins, rescued piggies, selected helpers, a piggy name and design if you make one, settings, and learning pace in its local app storage. This lets play continue where it left off. The app does not send this game data to BaseCodex or other companies. Android backup and device transfer of the app's data are disabled in this build. The data remains on the device until it is erased in the app or the app is uninstalled.

Network access and third parties

The Android app requests no internet or other Android permissions. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDK added by BaseCodex. It has no chat, cloud sync, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. Google Play handles any store purchase separately under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Deleting local game data

In Sky Piggies, tap Grown-ups, solve the addition problem shown on screen, then tap Privacy policy and data → Erase all game data and confirm. Uninstalling the app also removes its local game data. Sky Piggies has no account or server-side game profile to delete.
