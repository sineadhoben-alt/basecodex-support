Small Steps: English & Maths — privacy

iPhone, iPad and Android beta · Updated 29 October 2026
Developer: BaseCodex
Contact: transfertrainerni@gmail.com

What is saved

Small Steps saves child nicknames, school systems and years, avatars, starting-point responses, lesson attempts, writing, weekly plans and device preferences in private app storage. No child email address, date of birth or photo is needed. Use fictional profiles for community software testing.

Developer access

There is no Small Steps or ChatGPT account. Learning records are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app has no ads, analytics SDK, live AI service or microphone recording. The test build has no purchases or subscriptions. Optional support feedback is described below.

Private iCloud on iPhone and iPad

When available, the native app syncs family progress through the same Apple Account’s private CloudKit database. Apple manages the account and cloud service. The developer does not receive the Apple password or access to families’ private records through a developer learning-data server. Offline work is kept on the device and syncing requires an Internet connection. Apple Family Sharing alone does not share these records between different Apple Accounts. Apple processes account and cloud data under its privacy policy.

Optional private Google sync on Android

A parent can connect Google sync in the PIN-protected parent area on each device. Google asks the parent to choose an account and authorise Small Steps’ private application-data storage. This does not grant access to unrelated Google Drive files. Profiles, progress, avatars, unfinished work and plans sync through the same Google account. Google accounts and Apple iCloud do not share progress with one another. Offline learning works without connecting Google.

Google account separation

The selected Google account identifier and email are kept in private device settings to separate saved families. Short-lived access tokens are handled in memory and through Google Play services; they are not included in learning records, exported family backups or developer logs. A parent can disconnect and revoke access. Hidden cloud data remains until removed through Google’s app-data controls. Google processes account and Drive data under its own privacy policy.

Google API Limited Use

Small Steps’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. Google data is used for parent-requested family sync and account separation, not advertising, sale of data or training AI models.

Parent controls

A local six-digit PIN protects parent reports, family changes, learning settings and the feedback controls. It is stored as a salted digest, does not sync, and is a convenience control rather than encryption. Children choose their names and avatars without a PIN. Anyone using the device can choose any child profile; these are not separate secure child accounts. This beta has no forgotten-PIN recovery, so keep the PIN safe.

Parent-initiated feedback

Flag an activity opens a parent-only screen with the app build, content reference, content code and chosen issue category. It does not attach a child’s profile, answers, writing or family backup. Copying places that reference on the device clipboard. Preparing feedback opens an email draft, which the parent can review, edit, cancel or send. Nothing is emailed automatically. If you send feedback to transfertrainerni@gmail.com, we receive the sender address and the message you choose to send through Gmail. Leave out children’s personal information. Support correspondence is retained only while needed to investigate and resolve the enquiry; contact the same address to request its deletion.

Read-aloud, printing and backups

Read-aloud uses the device’s text-to-speech service for lesson explanations; the selected voice service may use network services under its own settings. Printing sends the chosen report to the system print service. Parents can export a family file through the system file picker. Selected printers, mail apps and document providers handle these actions under their own privacy settings. Keep exported copies private.

Control and deletion

Parents can remove a child profile in the parent area. Its removal is included in the next successful sync. Copies on other devices, exported files and provider backups require separate attention. Disconnecting sync does not delete existing cloud copies; reconnecting an account may restore saved progress. Removing app storage or uninstalling can remove unsynced local work. Manage remaining iCloud or Google app data through the relevant account’s storage controls.

Testing services and this website

Apple, Google and any selected testing provider process installation, review and tester-feedback information under their own policies. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub and the browser may process technical visit information such as an IP address. We will update this page before releasing changes to these data practices.
