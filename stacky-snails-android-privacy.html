Stacky Snails for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026 for Android app version 1.0.0 (1).

The game

Stacky Snails is a children's game. It does not ask you to create an account. It has no ads, analytics or in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Scores, stars, earned coins, unlocked outfits, selected garden and sound preference are saved on your device so you can continue playing. The game does not send this information to us or other servers. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this game. The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Deleting progress

Use Settings → Reset saved progress to delete saved game information. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it also removes local progress. There is no account to delete.

Support website and email

A parent or guardian may choose to open the support/privacy website from Settings. This opens an external browser. The browser and website host may process technical visit information, such as an IP address, under their own privacy practices. The support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you email support, we receive your email address and what you send so we can reply. Please do not include a child's name, school or other personal information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence. Email is handled through Gmail.

Changes and contact

If this policy or the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its review date before the change is released. For privacy questions, contact bumblebopgames@gmail.com.
